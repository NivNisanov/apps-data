How do I get into the Grown-ups area?

Tap Grown-ups at the top of the home screen. A grown-up gate appears first, and it also guards the Plus plans screen:

  1. Read the written instruction, for example “Hold the green circle for 3 seconds”. It is shown on screen but never read aloud, so young children can't follow it.
  2. Press and hold the circle in that colour until its ring fills up (3 seconds). Letting go early starts the count again.

The colours and the one to hold change every time. Tap Not now to close the gate.

How do I restore Tap & Count Plus?

If you bought Plus before, on this device or another one, you can get it back for free. Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID that made the purchase, then:

  1. Tap Grown-ups on the home screen and pass the grown-up gate.
  2. In the Tap & Count Plus card, tap Restore purchases.

Restore purchases is also at the bottom of the plans screen (Grown-ups → See plans). If nothing turns up, the app says “No purchases found for this Apple ID”. Check which Apple ID you're signed in with and try again.

How do I change or cancel my subscription?

Yearly and Monthly subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple, so you change or cancel them in your Apple account:

  1. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  2. Choose Tap & Count, then pick another plan or tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date (or before a free trial ends) so you aren't charged for the next period. While a subscription is active, the Grown-ups area also has a Manage subscription button that opens the same Apple screen.

The Forever (lifetime) plan is a one-time purchase. It never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Family Sharing: Plus can be shared with your Family Sharing group. Family members get it on their own devices when purchase sharing is on (Settings → your name → Family Sharing). For refunds, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com.

How does the daily play time limit work?

Each child can have their own daily limit. It is Off unless you set it.

  1. Open Grown-ups (pass the gate) and choose the child at the top.
  2. In the child's settings, under Daily play time, pick Off, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes.

The time played that day adds up. Once it reaches the limit, Pip goes to sleep and the home screen shows “See you tomorrow!” instead of the Play button. New questions wait until the next day.

A game that's already running is never cut off halfway. The limit takes effect the next time your child is back on the home screen.

How do I switch between English and Hebrew?

Tap & Count speaks English and Hebrew (עברית). At first it uses Hebrew if your device is set to Hebrew or to the Israel region, and English otherwise. You can choose a different language from your phone's, for example an English phone with a child who plays in Hebrew:

  • On the first screen after installing, use the language menu at the top.
  • Any time later: Grown-ups → Language, then choose English or עברית.

The words on screen and Pip's voice switch right away, and in Hebrew the app reads right to left. The choice is saved on each device, the same way iOS saves its own per-app language. So if your device lists more than one preferred language, Settings → Tap & Count → Language shows the same choice.

How does iCloud sync work?

Your children's profiles, progress, gardens, reports and daily limits sync between your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit private database). We never see this data. For sync to work:

  • Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on each device.
  • Keep iCloud turned on for Tap & Count (Settings → your name → iCloud).
  • Open the app. Changes arrive when the app opens or comes back to the front, and can take a moment.

To check, scroll to the bottom of the Grown-ups area. It says either “Progress syncs between your devices with iCloud.” or “Progress is saved on this device (iCloud is off or unavailable).” Without iCloud, everything still works and is saved on that device only. Language, sound and haptics settings are kept per device.
