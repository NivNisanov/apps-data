Privacy Policy

Tap & Count: Kids Math Games

Effective date: November 24, 2026Last updated: November 24, 2026iPhone & iPad

The short version

Tap & Count is a math app for children aged 4 to 8. We built it so that we never collect anything about your child or your family.

  • No ads, ever
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No personal data sent to us
  • Works offline
  • Grown-up gate on purchases, settings and links

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no servers of ours to send data to, and it contains no third-party code at all: no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or social SDKs. We (the developer) do not receive your child's name, progress, answers, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other information.

The app does not use the camera, the microphone, location, or the advertising identifier, and it never asks for them.

What the app keeps, and where

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information. It stays on your device and, if iCloud is turned on, in your own private iCloud account. We cannot see it.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, progress syncs between your family's iPhone and iPad through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) private database. That data belongs to your Apple account, is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and is not accessible to us. If iCloud is off, everything stays on the device only. The grown-ups area shows which mode the app is using.

In-app purchases

Tap & Count is free to try. Tap & Count Plus (yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) opens all the Plus math skills. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details; the app only learns whether Plus is active.

  • The purchase screen is behind a grown-up gate: an instruction shown in writing, never spoken, that asks a grown-up to hold a named colour for 3 seconds.
  • Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Plus can be shared with your Family Sharing group.

Other features, explained

Reminders (optional)

A grown-up can turn on a weekly report reminder or a reminder two days before a free trial ends. These are local notifications scheduled on your device; nothing is sent from our side. iOS asks for permission only when a grown-up turns one on, inside the gated grown-ups area.

Sharing the weekly card (optional)

In the grown-ups area you can tap “Share this week” to create a picture of your child's weekly card, which includes the name you gave them. It is only shared if you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet, and it goes only where you send it.

Pip's voice

Spoken instructions are pre-recorded clips built into the app, generated with AI text-to-speech before release. For languages without recorded clips, the app uses the voice built into iOS on the device. No audio is recorded and no text is sent anywhere.

Links

The only links out of the app (Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy) are in the grown-up areas, behind the grown-up gate.

Children's privacy

Tap & Count is made for children and is offered in the App Store's Kids category. We follow Apple's rules for kids apps: no third-party analytics or advertising, no data sent to third parties, and a grown-up gate before any purchase, link, or permission request.

We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from anyone else. There is no account to create, no chat, and no way for children to share information with others.

Europe and the UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): we do not process personal data about you or your child. Data kept on your device or in your iCloud account is under your control and can be deleted as described below.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove one child: grown-ups area → choose the child → Remove child. Their garden, progress and reports are deleted from the device and from iCloud.
  • Remove everything on a device: delete the app.
  • Remove synced iCloud data: iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → manage your storage, then choose Tap & Count.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the grown-ups area or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. We will never start collecting data without first updating this policy and releasing a new version of the app.
